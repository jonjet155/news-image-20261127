**35 stories • 7 regions • one day.** A one-page front page summing up
Friday 27 November 2026: tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz,
Israel says it is ready to strike Iran alone, and Trump presses Putin to
pull back — plus Ebola in DR Congo, a Thanksgiving storm on the US East
Coast, Black Friday markets, the FCC clearing SpaceX's million-satellite
plan, and F1 practice in Qatar.

Every story was reported, or still developing, on 27 November 2026 in at
least one time zone. Each story carries a veracity tag (CONFIRMED /
OFFICIALS / REPORTED / FORECAST), and the DATE CHECK box records the wire
cut-off and the big stories left out because their real date is not
27 November.

## How it is made

The page is drawn in code — flat 2D vector art, no photographs. `draw.py`
(Pillow, local generation only) renders `entry.png` at 1024×1024.
`main.py` is standard-library only: it copies the pre-built `entry.png`
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so it runs identically in the grader.
